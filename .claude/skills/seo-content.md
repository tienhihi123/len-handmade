# seo-content

Generate SEO-optimized Vietnamese marketing content for Len Handmade.

## When to invoke
- User asks "write product description"
- "Create blog post about..."
- "Generate meta tags for..."
- "Write SEO content"

## Process
1. **Understand target:**
   - Content type (product description, blog post, meta tags, category description)
   - Target keywords (if provided)
   - Product/topic details

2. **Read references (on-demand):**
   - `guides/CONTENT-STRATEGY.md` — Brand voice, tone guidelines
   - `guides/SEO-CHECKLIST.md` — SEO best practices

3. **Generate content with:**
   - **Language:** Natural Vietnamese (tiếng Việt tự nhiên)
   - **Tone:** Ấm áp, tinh tế, editorial luxury, gần gũi nhưng chuyên nghiệp
   - **Keywords:** Integrated naturally (NOT keyword stuffing)
   - **Length:**
     - Product descriptions: 150-300 words
     - Blog posts: 800-1,500 words
     - Meta descriptions: 120-160 characters
   - **Emotional touch:** Handmade, artisan, craft story elements

4. **Include SEO elements:**
   - Meta title (50-60 chars)
   - Meta description (120-160 chars)
   - H1, H2, H3 hierarchy
   - Target keywords in first 100 words
   - Internal linking suggestions (if blog post)

## Brand voice guidelines (quick reference)
- ✅ "Sản phẩm len thủ công tinh tế, mang lại cảm giác ấm áp"
- ✅ "Từng mũi đan tỉ mỉ, gửi gắm tâm huyết nghệ nhân"
- ❌ "Len handmade rẻ nhất", "Siêu khuyến mãi giảm giá"
- ❌ Tone quá casual hoặc quá formal/corporate

## References
- `guides/CONTENT-STRATEGY.md` — Full brand voice guide
- `guides/SEO-CHECKLIST.md` — Technical SEO rules

## Output format
```markdown
# [Title with keyword]

**Meta Title:** [50-60 chars]
**Meta Description:** [120-160 chars]

## Introduction (H2)
[Content with keyword in first 100 words...]

## Main Content (H2)
[Body paragraphs...]

## Conclusion (H2)
[Closing thoughts...]

---
**Keywords used:** keyword1, keyword2, keyword3
**Word count:** XXX words
```
